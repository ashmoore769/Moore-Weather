MOORE WEATHER

West Warwick, Queensland

Your weather.
One tap away.

Add Moore Weather to your home screen for a quick look at the weather, wherever you are.

Your browser will ask you to confirm.

On iPhone or iPad

  1. 1

    Open in Safari

    Visit Moore Weather in Safari. If you opened it inside Facebook, Messenger or another app, copy the address below and open it in Safari first.

  2. 2

    Tap Share

    Look for the square with an arrow pointing up. Depending on your Safari layout, open the page menu first.

  3. 3

    Add to Home Screen

    Choose Add to Home Screen, leave Open as Web App switched on if shown, then tap Add.

Can’t see “Add to Home Screen”?

Scroll further down the Share menu. Tap Edit Actions if needed and add Add to Home Screen to the list.

On Android

  1. 1

    Open in Chrome

    Visit Moore Weather in Chrome. If you’re viewing it inside another app, copy the address below and open it in Chrome first.

  2. 2

    Open the three-dot menu ⋮

    Tap Install and create shortcut, then Install. Some versions show Install app or Add to Home screen instead.

  3. 3

    Confirm, then you’re set

    Follow the prompt to add Moore Weather. Look for its icon on your home screen or in your app list.

No install option?

Use Create shortcut or Add to Home screen if offered. You can also bookmark the page and keep using it in your browser.

On your computer

  1. 1

    Look beside the address bar

    In Chrome or Edge, look for the install icon. If the button above is available, you can use that instead.

  2. 2

    Or use your browser’s menu

    Look for Install Moore Weather, Install app or Apps. On a Mac using Safari, choose File → Add to Dock if available.

  3. 3

    Keep it close by

    Open Moore Weather from your apps. You can pin its icon to your taskbar or Dock for quick access.

Prefer using your browser?

That works too. Bookmark Moore Weather and come back whenever you like.
